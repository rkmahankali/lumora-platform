About Sophie

Award-winning wedding and portrait photographer based in Shoreditch, London. I specialise in capturing authentic, unscripted moments — the laugh just before the kiss, the father wiping a tear, the stolen glance across the room.

My style blends editorial precision with a warm, documentary approach. I've shot over 200 weddings across the UK and Europe, and my work has been featured in Vogue Weddings, Rock My Wedding, and The Sunday Times Style.

Every booking includes a pre-shoot consultation, full edited gallery (delivered in 2 weeks), and lifetime access to your private Lumora gallery.
